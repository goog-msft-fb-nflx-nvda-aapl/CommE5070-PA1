Cheapest way to test whether AF3's Task 2 edge (58.8% vs our 47.1%) transfers into our own pipeline. **Done 2026-09-18** (`src/af3_stack.py`): weighted-sum sweep over both AF3 prompts x every available frozen/fine-tuned backbone (MERT, MuQ, fine-tuned MERT), both datasets, not just Task 2. **B: MuQ+AF3(cot_then_answer, w=0.4) → top1=0.6569/top3=0.8824, new overall best, beats AF3 alone (0.588) and MuQ alone (0.500).** **A: MuQ+AF3(direct, w=0.5) → top1=0.5530/top3=0.8485, also a new overall best**, beats the 3-way ensemble (0.5227). ## Improvement queue, round 3 (from 4-way deep research on research_prompt_round3.txt — see IMPROVEMENT_FINDINGS_ROUND3.md for full reconciliation)
- [x] **Task 2: OOF-fitted, temperature-calibrated fusion** — refit the AF3+MuQ fusion weight using 5-fold out-of-fold predictions on the 798-sample training set (not the 102-sample validation sweep), with AF3's probabilities temperature-calibrated (fit on train via NLL) before combining. Highest cross-source convergence (3-4 of 4 sources); directly targets the open "fusion vs. AF3-alone" significance gap (currently p=0.21). **Done 2026-09-18** (`src/af3_stack_oof.py`): **major finding — the original 0.657 result does NOT reproduce under a leakage-free refit.** NLL-optimal OOF fusion: top1=0.5686 (barely above AF3-alone 0.5588, McNemar p=1.0, n01=1/n10=0). Accuracy-optimal OOF fusion: top1=0.5882 (exactly ties AF3-alone on top1, only top3 improves). **Conclusion: the 0.657 headline number was validation-set overfitting in the weight-sweep, not a real effect** — revised honest Task 2 number is ≈0.588 (matching AF3 zero-shot alone). Full results in WORKLOG.md's round-3 section; "CURRENT overall-best configs" table updated. **Task 1's 0.553 point estimate also re-tested** (same script, corrected to the actual A probe config: layer1+SVM, not B's layer2+logreg — a bug caught before trusting the first run): OOF-refit gives top1=0.5379 (NLL-optimal), only a 1.5pt drop from the original 0.553 — **Task 1's fusion result largely survives the leakage-free refit, in clear contrast to Task 2's collapse.** Significantly beats AF3-alone (p=0.0055); not significantly better than the probe alone (p=0.29). Report 0.538 as the more defensible Task 1 number going forward. Full comparison in WORKLOG.md.
- [x] **Task 1: CORAL ordinal head on best frozen probe backbone** — replace the nominal 6-way softmax with a CORAL (Cao et al. 2019, arXiv:1901.07884) cumulative-link ordinal head on the winning MuQ/MERT layer. Highest cross-source convergence for Task 1 (all 4 sources); directly tests whether the nominal-vs-ordinal decision surface is why 4 of our top configs are statistically tied at n=132. **Done 2026-09-19** (`src/coral_ordinal.py`): **clear negative result.** CORAL top1=0.318 vs. capacity-matched linear logreg 0.500 (p=0.0005) and established SVM 0.508 (p=0.0002) — loses on every metric including the ordinal-specific ones (MAE 1.18 vs 0.79, QWK 0.63 vs 0.75). Verified not a training artifact via a 9-config epoch×lr sweep — val accuracy never left a narrow 0.28-0.34 band. Directly contradicts all 4 round-3 sources' top recommendation for Task 1; reported as a genuine, hyperparameter-robust negative finding, queue item closed (no further CORAL/CORN tuning planned).
